# nosey_parker Strategies and Tactics

## Workflow sequence

1. Discover with broad scan.
2. Prioritize high-risk detectors.
3. Validate context and deduplicate.
4. Convert validated findings to nugget arrays.
5. Respond with rotate/revoke/remove actions.

## Practical tactics

- Phase scans for large repositories.
- Prioritize exploitable credentials over generic tokens.
- Re-scan post-remediation and compare deltas.
